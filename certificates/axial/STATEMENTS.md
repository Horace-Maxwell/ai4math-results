## 2. Checklist notes (formal-conjectures style)

- (a) **Sets vs families** in sum decompositions: a family `(A_i)` with repetitions would force `A_iA_i = 0` for repeated members; the set version quantifies over sets, and "every sum decomposition (set) contains `⊤`" implies the family version (take the range). So `OnlyTrivialSumDecompositions` is at least as strong as the source notion.
- (b) **Trivially true / vacuous risks.** `IsPrimitiveAxialAlgebra` is a conjunction of positive facts; the global propositions are `∀ … , hyp → concl` and we prove their negations by exhibiting an instance, so there is no vacuity risk in what we claim. `Dominance_nonsymmetric` is an `∃` with a strict inequality — not satisfiable by a degenerate choice (`<` needs a strict inclusion).
- (c) **`fromRel` symmetrisation** could add edges `p ~ q` when `μ q p ≠ 0` but `μ p q = 0`; impossible for commutative `μ`, which is part of `IsPrimitiveAxialAlgebra`.
- (d) **Eigenvalue bookkeeping.** `IsAxis` asks the fusion inclusion only for `λ, μ ∈ F.carrier`; the concrete laws' `star` is defined for all of `K` by `if`-cascades whose values on the carrier are exactly the tables (reviewers: evaluate the nine entries). Extra eigenvalues outside `F` are excluded by `eigspSet μ a F.carrier = ⊤`.
- (e) **`a ≠ 0`** in `IsAxis` matches [GS]/[MSZ] ("non-zero idempotent"); harmless.
- (f) **Decomposable vs sum decomposition.** These are different notions ([MSZ] vs [KMS]); both are formalized and both are refuted/used as the sources intend. (A central extension of `3C(η) ⊕ 3C(η)` — see the report — shows they really differ; it is not in the challenge.)
- (g) **Universe level.** Global statements quantify over `Type`; mathematically the counterexamples are countable-dimensional anyway.
- (h) **What is NOT stated**: nothing about Seress/graded laws, Monster type, Majorana algebras; Frobenius forms occur only in the existence conjunct of Theorem C.
- (i) **Public wording** follows `REVIEW-CHALLENGE.md` §10: always "for arbitrary fusion laws" (here non-Seress, only trivially graded); never "first" or "in every reading"; [GS] 3.9 is credited to [KMP] (2022) and Peng (2026); "statements formalized in Lean 4; proofs in progress" until phase C passes.
